Smart Canteen Food Waste System

IoT devices measure what gets cooked, served and thrown away. AI models use that data to tell the kitchen how much to cook the next day, and any surplus goes to students at a discount or to a local NGO.

Workflow

Input

Smart bins weigh and photograph discarded food, counter scales record servings, and storage sensors track temperature. The canteen logs what it cooked and sold. Students pre-book and rate meals, and the system pulls in the academic calendar and weather.

Processing

Edge devices send readings over MQTT to the cloud, where the data is cleaned and stored. Computer vision identifies wasted dishes, the forecasting model predicts next-day demand per dish, and anomaly detection flags days with unusually high waste.

Output

The kitchen sees how much to cook each morning. Near closing, students get alerts about discounted portions, and an NGO is notified to collect what's left. Weekly reports show which dishes are wasted most so the menu can change.

Technology

IoT hardwareLoad-cell smart bins, bin camera, counter scales, DHT22 sensors, ESP32 / Raspberry Pi
ConnectivityMQTT over Wi-Fi, AWS IoT Core
AI / MLYOLO for food recognition, XGBoost / LSTM for forecasting, Isolation Forest for anomalies
Backend & dataFastAPI, PostgreSQL, cloud object storage for images
AppsFlutter student app, React kitchen dashboard
NotificationsFirebase Cloud Messaging for students and NGO partners
