The model is a ridge regression for each dish, using day of week, exam weeks, events, rain and the number of pre-bookings from the student app. It is trained on 8 weeks of canteen records and adds a 6% buffer so the counter doesn't run out. You can edit the pre-booking numbers to see the forecast change.

    How the app connects to the system

    • Each pre-booking adds to the "Pre-booked" count on the kitchen plan, and the forecast updates right away.
    • When the live service finds food left over near closing, a discount alert appears in Notifications here.
    • Ratings show up in Reports next to each dish's waste, so the canteen can tell unpopular dishes from oversized ones.
    • In a real deployment students log in with their college email, and an ID card swipe at the counter gives the daily headcount.
